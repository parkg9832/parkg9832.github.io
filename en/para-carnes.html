PRODUCT DETAIL · SSAMJANG

PARA CARNES

A Korean sauce inspired by ssamjang, made to bring deep umami to meat and vegetables.

Flavor profile

Mild heat with deep umami, doenjang flavor and toasted notes.

Doenjang, gochujang, sesame and shiitake

Doenjang and gochujang create a savory fermented base. Toasted sesame, sesame oil and shiitake extract add umami and toasted notes.

For dipping cooked meat

Pair it with grilled, steamed or boiled meat, chicken and anticuchos. It also goes with vegetables. Cook the meat, dip and enjoy.

It began with a question

Salsa Coreana grew from the everyday Latin American meals its founder experienced firsthand. Seeing sauces served with chicken, meat, snacks and everyday food sparked a question: what if Korean flavors could join these familiar meals?
