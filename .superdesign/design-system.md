# MOKDA public website design system

MOKDA is a Korean K-Food brand connecting Korean food culture with everyday life in Latin America. The website should feel warm, youthful, confident, and food-led—not corporate, ornamental, luxury-coded, or template-like.

## Brand foundation

- Primary message: `Comer Corea / 한국을 먹다`.
- Brand essence: food connects cultures.
- Voice: close, easy, clear, human. Avoid stiff corporate clichés.
- Brand story remains founder-led: MOKDA began with one Korean founder's 2024 journey to Latin America and his experience sharing meals with Latin American friends.

## Visual language

- Use only the existing palette: MOKDA Green `#006943`, MOKDA Orange `#E8561D` (or existing web orange `#ef5f18`), deep brown `#321506`, cream `#fff8ef`, warm sand `#f8dfc4`/`#f7ddc2`, white, black.
- Brand green and orange are accents, not competing full-page backgrounds.
- Keep the current fonts: Archivo Black for Latin display, Noto Sans/Noto Sans KR for body and Korean, Bebas Neue for compact labels.
- Preserve the real global header/footer and current overall brand identity.
- Prefer editorial composition, thin rules, strong photography, asymmetric but orderly grids, and generous negative space.
- Avoid generic dashboard cards, heavy drop shadows, oversized rounded white boxes, gradients unrelated to the existing image overlays, extra colors, decorative serif fonts, and excessive pill shapes.
- Use large display type selectively. Section headings must not overwhelm the content or look like presentation slides.
- Photography should carry narrative meaning: shared food, founder journey, Korean ingredients, and the bridge between Korean and Latin American tables.

## About-page direction

- Keep the existing hero identity, but improve the narrative rhythm below it.
- Expand the Brand Story into an editorial sequence with the founder as the subject.
- Use the exact brandbook meaning: spring 2024 journey with one bag; sharing meals with Latin American friends; discovering food as a direct way to understand a culture and its people; Korean ingredients, a young perspective, and today's Korean sensibility brought closer to Latin American tables.
- Redesign Vision/Mission/Essence without floating cards: use editorial columns, fine dividers, restrained numbering, and one confident essence statement.
- Responsive behavior must remain polished at 1440px desktop and around 390px mobile, with no horizontal overflow.
- Keep motion restrained to the existing reveal behavior and respect reduced-motion settings.
